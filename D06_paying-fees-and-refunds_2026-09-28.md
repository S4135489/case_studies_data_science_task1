# Paying your fees and refunds (overview)

## Payments

A number of payment options are available for local and international students. You can also set up a payment schedule if you'd like to pay your fees in instalments, or choose to apply your payment to specific fees and charges.

## Refunds

You may apply for a refund if you have a credit balance in your student account due to:

- changes to your enrolment load
- withdrawal from your program
- an approved leave of absence, or
- overpayment.

# Fee refunds (detail page)

How to apply for a refund as a domestic or continuing international student.

Note: this page is for local (domestic) students and continuing international students. An international student who has not accepted their offer, or who cancels their first semester before the relevant census date, is considered a commencing international student and should refer to refund guidelines for commencing students (separate process, see "Refunds for commencing or newly enrolled international students").

## Eligibility

To be eligible for a refund, you must have a **credit balance** in your RMIT student account. A credit balance results when the total sum of payments exceeds the total fees for a specific term.

If you make changes to your enrolment **before the census date**, this may reduce your fees for that term and may result in a credit balance. These changes include:

- reducing your study load, e.g. from full-time to part-time
- withdrawing from your program, or
- taking an approved leave of absence.

**Changes to enrolment made after census date will not result in a credit balance.**

Important: if you are an international student, it's a condition of your student visa that you complete your program within its expected duration (the end date of your current Confirmation of Enrolment, CoE, issued by RMIT). You should remain enrolled in a full-time study load throughout your program.

## Check your account balance

1. Log in to Enrolment Online with your RMIT email address and password.
2. Select **Finances**.
3. View **Finance Summary**.

A negative balance (e.g. -$220.00) means you have a credit balance. A positive balance (e.g. $220.00) indicates a due fee or charge.

Overpayments will be assessed case-by-case and may not be refundable, depending on the circumstances (page excerpt cuts off here).

# Refunds for commencing or newly enrolled international students (related page, brief excerpt)

- If a refund application is successful, any outstanding charges and administrative fees are deducted from the refundable amount.
- If fees were paid by credit card, the approved refund is processed back to the same card. Otherwise, it's paid to the nominated bank account.
- Correct and complete refund applications are processed within **28 business days**; transfers to overseas bank accounts can take up to 6 weeks.
- RMIT is not responsible for currency variations in foreign exchange rates.

## Note on other campuses

RMIT Vietnam has separate, different fee, refund and payment processes (e.g. non-refundable deposits, different remission-of-debt process). Do not use RMIT Vietnam pages (rmit.edu.vn) for this knowledge base.
