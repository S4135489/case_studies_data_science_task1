# Apply from Australia

If you currently live in Australia and are applying to RMIT as an international student, the way you apply will depend on your chosen course.

Select your course type to find out which application method you need.

- To apply for RMIT Foundation Studies, use RMIT's online application service.
- To apply for an English language course, use RMIT's online application service.
- Vocational study (TAFE), including Certificates, Diplomas (excluding the Diploma of Commerce, see Undergraduate below), and Advanced Diplomas: use RMIT's online application service.

## Undergraduate qualifications

If you are applying for an undergraduate qualification, the application method depends on the completion date of one of the following:

- an international student currently completing an Australian Year 12 in Australia; or
- an International Baccalaureate (in Australia or New Zealand).

**Apply via VTAC:** If you complete before **13 January 2026**, you must apply via the Victorian Tertiary Admissions Centre (VTAC).

**Apply direct to RMIT:** If you complete on or after **13 January 2026**, you must apply using RMIT's online application service.

If you are applying for a course not listed above, use RMIT's online application service.

## Applying from overseas?

If you are applying as an international student and are not in Australia, use RMIT's comprehensive guide to apply (separate page).

## Application dates

Course application dates can vary depending on the RMIT course you are applying for.

## Need help?
